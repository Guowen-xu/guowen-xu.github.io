<p align="justify">  <font face="Times New Roman" color=black size=4> I am currently a full professor with the School of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Chengdu, China. Prior to joining UESTC, I was a Postdoc research fellow at City University of Hong Kong and a  Research  Fellow at Nanyang Technolgoical University. My current research interests focus on  AI Security and Privacy. </font> </p>

<p align="justify"> <font face="Times New Roman" color=black size=4> I have published over 100 CCF-A papers in reputable security conferences/journals, such as IEEE S&P, ACM CCS, USENIX Security, ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, TDSC and TIFS. Currently, I serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE TPAMI, TDSC, TIFS, TCSVT, TNSM, TASLP and ACM Computing Survey. Moreover, I have had the privilege of participating as a Technical Program Committee (TPC) member over 30 top tier conferences such as ICML(Area Chair), NeurIPS(Area Chair), ICLR(Area Chair), KDD (Area Chair), AAAI(Area Chair),  CSCW (Associate Chair), CHI (Associate Chair),USENIX Security, ACM CCS, CVPR,  WWW, etc.</font> </p>

<p align="justify"> <font face="Times New Roman" color=black size=4> My research has been recognized with several prestigious awards, including the IEEE-HKN Frank and Shelley Chang Outstanding Young Scholar Award (2026), the IEEE Signal Processing Society Best Paper Award (2025), the IEEE Region 10 Young Professionals Outstanding Volunteer in Academia Award (2025), the Young Researcher Award from the IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Society Technical Committee on Homeland Security (2025), IEEE Computer Society Computing’s Top 30 Early Career Professionals (2025), and the Wu Wenjun First Prize for Artificial Intelligence Science and Technology Progress (2022).</font> </p>
